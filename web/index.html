        Banishment

        At level 100 you may sacrifice your character to the Abyss. Banishing resets your character to level 1, but permanently grants +1 Banishment (Depth) and carries a slice of your current stats into your next life. The more times you've already Banished, the more of your run survives the next one.

        • Banishments 0 – 90.25% retained
        • Banishments 10 – 490.50% retained
        • Banishments 50 – 990.75% retained
        • Banishments 100+100% retained

        What You'll Gain

        • Banishments (Depth)0 → 1
        • Power carried forward20
        • Defense carried forward15
        • Max HP carried forward75

        Everything else resets: Level back to 1, XP and Gold to 0, and all inventory/equipment cleared. The stats above are the level-100 floor (20 Power / 15 Defense / 75 Max HP) plus your current Banishment tier's cut of whatever you've grown past that floor -- the further past level 100 you push a life, the more this next life starts with.
