      Banishment

      At level 100 you may sacrifice your character to the Abyss. Banishing resets your character to level 1, but permanently grants Abyssal Prowess and carries a slice of your current stats into your next life. The more Abyssal Prowess you've banked from past banishments, the more of your run survives the next one.

      • Abyssal Prowess 100 – 5000.50% retained
